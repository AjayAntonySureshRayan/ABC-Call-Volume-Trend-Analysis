# Project Background 
The objectives of the ABC Call Volume Trend Analysis project are to analyze inbound call trends over the 23-day period to identify peak and low-traffic periods for optimizing resource allocation. The project aims to evaluate customer wait times by measuring queue durations and identifying patterns that may contribute to longer waits and customer dissatisfaction. It also seeks to assess call outcomes by categorizing calls as answered, abandoned, or transferred, in order to understand factors affecting first-call resolution. Monitoring and improving agent performance is another key objective, focusing on call handling time and resolution rates, while identifying high-performing agents and areas for targeted training. The project is intended to provide actionable insights to enhance customer experience and operational efficiency, recommending improvements in staffing, call routing, and process automation using AI-powered tools such as IVR, RPA, and predictive analytics. Additionally, the analysis supports business decision-making by correlating call trends with marketing or advertising campaigns to understand their impact on inbound inquiries and identify cost-effective strategies for converting inquiries into loyal customers. Overall, the project enables data-driven optimization of the inbound support process, helping the CX team proactively manage call volumes and maintain high service standards.

Insights and Recommendations are provided on the following Key areas :

**Call Volume Trends:** Analyzed inbound call patterns over 23 days to identify peak and low-traffic periods. Recommendations focus on optimizing staffing and forecasting call volumes.

**Queue Time & Efficiency:** Assessed wait times and call durations to understand customer experience. Recommendations include intelligent routing, IVR enhancements, and agent training.

**Call Outcome Analysis:** Evaluated answered, abandoned, and transferred calls to identify satisfaction bottlenecks. Recommendations focus on reducing transfers and abandoned calls through process improvements and automation.

**Agent Performance:** Reviewed individual agent metrics to identify top performers and areas for improvement. Recommendations include targeted coaching and leveraging best practices.
